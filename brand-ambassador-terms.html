Terms & Conditions

Become an AIK Digital Brand Ambassador and earn rewards by referring friends and family. Please review the campaign terms below.

  1. 1. Any individual is authorized to market and facilitate the opening of aik Digital consumer accounts for their friends and family on behalf of aik Digital.
  2. 2. An individual who successfully refers and facilitates the onboarding of at least one consumer account (other than their own account) will qualify as a Brand Ambassador.
  3. 3. Upon qualifying as a Brand Ambassador, the individual will receive a referral commission of PKR 200 for each successfully onboarded consumer account referred by them, including the first successful referral.
  4. 4. The Brand Ambassador must provide their Aik Consumer Account Number in the referral field while onboarding a referred individual.
  5. 5. Referral commission will be applicable for both Current and Savings account acquisitions.
  6. 6. A referred account will qualify for commission once at least one credit or debit transaction of PKR 50 or above is successfully completed within the same calendar month as the account acquisition.
  7. 7. Eligible referral commissions will be processed 7-10 working days after the end of the respective month.
  8. 8. Commission will be credited to the Brand Ambassador's AIK consumer account after deduction of applicable taxes.
